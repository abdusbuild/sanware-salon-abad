PRIVACY POLICY

How we handle your information

Last updated: 27 October 2026

1. Who we are

This website is operated by Sanware Salon (“we”, “us”), 2nd Floor, Canara Bank Building, MG Road, Near Khadim Showroom, Aurangabad, Bihar 824101. Phone/WhatsApp: +91 95255 99666.

2. Information we collect

This website has no user accounts and does not store personal data on a server. We may receive information when you:

  • Use a booking form: the details you enter (name, phone, service, preferred date, message) are placed into a WhatsApp message on your own device. Nothing is sent until you choose to send it on WhatsApp.
  • Call or message us: we receive your phone number and any information you share.
  • Visit the salon: we may note your name, phone number and service history to serve you better (for example skin or hair sensitivities).

3. How we use your information

  • To confirm, reschedule or remind you about appointments
  • To provide services safely, for example noting allergies or skin sensitivities
  • To answer your questions and send quotes or price lists you request
  • Occasionally, to share offers, only if you've agreed to receive them

We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone.

4. Photographs

We may photograph completed looks for our portfolio, website and social media only with your consent. If you'd like a photo of you removed, contact us and we'll take it down promptly.

5. Third-party services

Our website uses a few third-party services that have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Fonts to display typefaces
  • Google Maps, an embedded map on the Contact page
  • WhatsApp (Meta), for booking messages and chat
  • Instagram, through links to our profile

These services may collect technical data such as your IP address when their content loads.

6. Cookies & storage

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser keeps a small session flag so the booking popup isn't shown on every page. Embedded third-party content (like Google Maps) may set its own cookies.

7. Data retention & security

We keep appointment-related information only as long as needed to serve you and meet legal obligations, and take reasonable care to protect it.

8. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop sending you messages, at any time. Just call or WhatsApp +91 95255 99666. We handle personal data in line with applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

9. Children

Our website is not directed at children under 18. Services for minors should be booked by a parent or guardian.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “last updated” date above shows when it was last changed.
