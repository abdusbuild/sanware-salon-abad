TERMS & CONDITIONS

Simple rules that keep appointments running smoothly

Last updated: 27 October 2026

1. About these terms

By booking a service or using this website you agree to these terms. Please read them along with our Privacy Policy.

2. Appointments

  • Appointments are confirmed only after we reply to your call or WhatsApp message.
  • Please arrive 5–10 minutes early. If you're more than 15 minutes late we may need to shorten or reschedule your service.
  • Please give at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule.

3. Bridal & event bookings

Bridal and event dates may require an advance payment to block the date. Advance amounts, cancellation terms and any travel charges for on-location makeup will be shared and agreed with you at the time of booking.

4. Prices

Prices vary with hair length, products and the look chosen. Prices shared over phone or WhatsApp are estimates until confirmed in person, and may change without prior notice.

5. Health, allergies & patch tests

Please tell us about any allergies, skin conditions, pregnancy, recent treatments or medications before your service. For colour and chemical treatments we may recommend a patch or strand test. We're not responsible for reactions to conditions that were not disclosed.

6. Results & aftercare

Results of hair and skin treatments vary with hair and skin type, history and aftercare. Please follow the aftercare advice we give you. If you're unhappy with a service, tell us within 3 days and we'll do our best to put it right.

7. Photographs

We may ask to photograph your look for our portfolio. We only publish photos with your consent, and you can ask us to remove them at any time.

8. Website content

All photos and content on this website belong to Sanware Salon or its artists and may not be reused without permission. Hours, services and prices may change.
