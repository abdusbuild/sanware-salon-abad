Our Story संवारे

Sanware (संवारे) means to adorn, to make beautiful. We started Sanware Salon with a simple idea: everyone in Aurangabad deserves big-city quality in makeup, hair and nails, with warm, honest service and fair prices.

Founded and run by women, our studio on the 2nd floor of the Canara Bank building, near Khadim Showroom, is bright, spotless and calm. It's a place where brides, students, working women and mothers can relax and be looked after.

Our makeup work is led by artist Juhi Jaiswal (@glamourholic_juhi), known for looks that enhance your natural features without overdoing them.

Artists Who Listen

Our stylists and makeup artists are trained in the latest techniques: HD bridal makeup, permanent straightening, keratin, hair botox and nail extensions. But what clients value most is how comfortable they feel in the chair.

"Mam was looking professional, I was very comfortable throughout the process," wrote one client after her straightening treatment. That's the experience we aim for every single time.
